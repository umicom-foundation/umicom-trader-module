Read volume-weighted averages and price bands

The chart study selector includes rolling VWMA, Bollinger bands and a Donchian channel alongside SMA and EMA. The calculations belong to Framework, so the same bars and settings produce the same data in every frontend.

  1. Open a Trader chart and choose the instrument whose bars you want to inspect. Check that its data source and timeframe are the ones you intend to use.
  2. Choose Rolling VWMA, Bollinger bands or Donchian channel in the study selector.
  3. Set the period between 2 and 200. The period counts displayed timeframe candles, not source ticks or elapsed minutes.
  4. Wait for at least one complete window of bars. A line segment needs two valid adjacent results; fewer bars can therefore show no line.
  5. Zoom or pan. The study still uses the full retained history before the result is clipped to the visible chart. Changing timeframe recomputes it from the aggregated candles.
  • Rolling VWMA: closing prices weighted by their reported candle volumes. A window with no volume creates a gap. This is not session VWAP, anchored VWAP, or a tick-level execution benchmark.
  • Bollinger bands: the mean close plus and minus two population standard deviations. The centre is amber and the boundaries are blue.
  • Donchian channel: the highest high and lowest low within the window, including the current candle, with their midpoint as the centre.

If a line is missing, check the history length and volume. Boundaries outside the visible price scale are clipped. Non-finite prices, invalid candles or unrepresentable bands are rejected; they are not silently replaced with zeroes. The rolling window starts within retained chart history and does not infer missing bars or exchange sessions. Only one study choice is displayed at a time, and this setting is workspace state rather than a new persisted chart preference.

Changing a study does not submit, cancel or amend an order. These chart calculations do not establish a working live broker connection or market-data entitlement.

Read volume at bar close

  1. Select Volume at bar close in the chart study selector.
  2. Use the period control to choose between 2 and 200 price buckets. In this mode it is a bucket count, not a moving window.
  3. Read the horizontal bars on the right of the chart. Framework assigns each retained candle's volume to the bucket containing that candle's closing price. This approximation does not identify the actual prices of individual trades or separate buying from selling.
  4. The control line marks the middle of the largest bucket. The shaded value area grows from that bucket towards the larger adjacent volume until it covers at least 70% of the total. Ties choose the lower price; whole buckets can take coverage above 70%.
  5. Pan or zoom to inspect the chart: the profile still uses the entire retained history. Changing the timeframe changes the candles used by this approximation. The on-chart label reminds you which calculation is shown.

No positive volume means no control or value-area levels. Equal closing prices produce one bucket. If a range is too narrow for the chosen number of distinct floating-point edges, reduce the bucket count. These studies do not send orders. For individual trade data, Framework also provides UmiChartVolumeProfileCompute, which accepts price and volume observations directly.
